# Patch Management Report

## Overview
Patch management is the process of identifying, prioritizing, testing, and deploying updates to systems and applications. Effective patching reduces vulnerabilities, improves stability, and ensures compliance.

## Patch Management Lifecycle

1. **Asset Inventory**  
   Maintain an accurate list of hardware and software.

2. **Vulnerability Scanning**  
   Identify missing patches and known vulnerabilities.

3. **Patch Prioritization**  
   Rank patches based on severity, exploitability, and business impact.

4. **Testing**  
   Validate patches in a staging environment to avoid disruptions.

5. **Deployment**  
   Roll out patches across production systems.

6. **Verification & Reporting**  
   Confirm successful installation and document results.

## Risks of Poor Patch Management
- Exploitation of known vulnerabilities  
- Malware infections  
- Data breaches  
- System instability  
- Compliance failures and audit findings  

## Best Practices
- Automate patch deployment where possible  
- Maintain a consistent patch schedule  
- Prioritize critical CVEs and zero-day vulnerabilities  
- Test patches before deployment  
- Document changes for audit and compliance  

## Common Tools
- WSUS / SCCM  
- Ansible  
- Qualys / Nessus  
- Linux package managers (APT/YUM)  

## Conclusion
Timely patching is one of the most effective ways to reduce risk. A structured, repeatable patch management process ensures systems remain secure and compliant.
