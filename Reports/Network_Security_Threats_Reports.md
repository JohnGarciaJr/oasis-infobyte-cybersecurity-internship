# Network Security Threats Report

## Overview
Network security is essential for protecting organizational assets, maintaining service availability, and preventing unauthorized access. This report outlines common network-based threats, indicators of compromise, and recommended mitigation strategies.

## Common Network Threats

### Malware & Ransomware
Malicious software designed to disrupt operations, steal data, or encrypt systems for ransom. These threats often spread through phishing emails, drive‑by downloads, or vulnerable services.

### Phishing & Social Engineering
Attackers trick users into revealing credentials or installing malware. Email remains the most common delivery method.

### Man‑in‑the‑Middle (MITM) Attacks
Adversaries intercept or alter traffic between two parties, often through insecure Wi‑Fi networks or compromised routers.

### Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS)
Attackers overwhelm a target with excessive traffic, causing downtime and service disruption.

### Unauthorized Access
Weak passwords, misconfigurations, and exposed services allow attackers to enter systems without permission.

## Indicators of Compromise (IoCs)
- Unusual outbound traffic  
- Unknown processes or services running  
- Repeated failed login attempts  
- Unexpected port scanning activity  
- Sudden spikes in resource usage  

## Mitigation Strategies
- Implement network segmentation  
- Enforce strong authentication and MFA  
- Deploy firewalls, IDS/IPS, and endpoint protection  
- Patch systems regularly  
- Monitor logs and network traffic continuously  

## Conclusion
A layered defense strategy is essential for reducing risk. By combining technical controls, monitoring, and user awareness, organizations can significantly limit exposure to network threats.
